# Introduction

Camera based products are more and more popular in your nowaday life. Dash or Driver Monitoring cameras are mandatory accessories on a car. ADAS has been gradually becoming a standard in automotive industry. And robotaxi brings fantastic experiences to users. 

If you are a newbie to camera, this is a good starting point for your. 

The sample here uses Gstreamer (one of the most famous media libraries until now) for its implementation. It also introduces how to use shared memory to share camera captured frames across system, which is one of basic but the most efficient and popular technique.
